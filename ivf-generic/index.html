Clinical Services Offered

Fertility evaluation

Comprehensive clinical and diagnostic assessment to identify underlying factors affecting conception.

Female fertility care

Evaluation and medical management for ovulation disorders, PCOS, endometriosis, diminished ovarian reserve, and other reproductive health conditions.

Male fertility care

Diagnostic evaluation and clinical management for low sperm count, motility concerns, hormonal factors, and other male infertility conditions.

IVF (In Vitro Fertilisation)

In Vitro Fertilisation procedures conducted under standardised clinical and embryology laboratory protocols.

ICSI

Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection (ICSI) assisted fertilisation techniques for clinically indicated fertility conditions.

IUI

Intrauterine Insemination (IUI) procedures offered where clinically indicated based on diagnostic assessment.

Fertility preservation

Oocyte (egg) freezing and fertility preservation services for medical or planned future family building.

Frequently asked questions

When should we consult a fertility specialist?

If you've been trying to conceive for 12 months (or 6 months if the woman is over 35), it's advisable to seek clinical evaluation.

Will both partners need testing?

Yes. Fertility concerns may involve male factors, female factors, or both. Evaluation helps determine the most appropriate treatment.

Is IVF the first treatment option?

Not always. Depending on your diagnosis, treatment may begin with lifestyle changes, medication, IUI, or other fertility treatments before IVF is recommended.

How long does IVF treatment take?

The treatment timeline varies based on your personalised plan. Your fertility specialist will explain each stage during your consultation.

Are fertility consultations confidential?

Yes. All medical records and personal details are handled in accordance with patient confidentiality standards and applicable data protection laws.

How are consultation and treatment fees structured?

Consultation charges and procedure estimates are shared transparently at the hospital desk following clinical evaluation, based on the specific diagnostic tests or protocols medically indicated.
